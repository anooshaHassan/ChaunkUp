# Pitch
ChaunkUp checks messages and screenshots for scams in English, Urdu and Roman Urdu, explains verdicts plainly, and lets users alert trusted family contacts with one tap.

# Users
Everyday smartphone users.
Parents and elders.

# MVP features
1. Paste-text analysis with verdict and confidence.
2. Red flags highlighted in the original text.
3. Explanations in English, Urdu and Roman Urdu.
4. Screenshot upload.
5. Circle contacts with one-tap alert.
6. History of past checks.

# Out of scope
Voice notes, trending scams board, safety score, login, real SMS sending.

# Success checks
When a user checks scam text, they see a verdict, confidence and highlighted red flags.
When a user uploads a readable screenshot, they receive a verdict and can view explanations in English, Urdu and Roman Urdu.
When a user taps Alert my Circle on a Scam or Suspicious result, a WhatsApp or email share link opens with the message and verdict, and the check appears in history.
